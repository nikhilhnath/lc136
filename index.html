Economic Loss & ROB Payback

Based on Indian Roads Congress (IRC:SP:30) guidelines, 9,100 commuter-hours are lost daily. Factoring the Value of Time (VOT) at ₹320/hr, the community loses ₹29.1 Lakhs daily in productivity.

Financial Conclusion: Combined fuel and time losses cost the state ₹112 Crores annually. A standard 2-lane ROB costs ~₹45 Crores. Constructing the bridge recovers its capital cost in just 4.8 months.
