PHASE 1 MILESTONES ACHIEVED (Aug 2026)
Following persistent community pressure, civic bodies launched the Enforced Easy Flow Corridor (EEFC) pilot:
  • Flexible Lane Dividers & Barricades: Installed to enforce lane discipline, reducing peak queue clearance significantly.
  • Footpath Drain Covers: Successfully repaired to ensure safe, unbroken walking routes.

We urgently demand the installation of metal safety bollards to protect the 12,000+ vulnerable school children navigating this junction. The ₹45 Cr ROB remains the only permanent solution.
LC 136 Chronology & Official Project Audit

A neutral retrospective chronicle of administrative milestones, technical evaluations, and infrastructure planning compiled exclusively from official public records, statutory project sanctions, and K-RIDE tender disclosures.

JULY 2026
Phased Commissioning & Review
Ongoing administrative and financial reviews of the 48-km track doubling works note phased project commissioning targets extending into 2027, with total utilized project outlays reported at approximately ₹688.8 crore.
MAY 13, 2026
Corridor-4 Tender Publication
K-RIDE formally publishes comprehensive e-procurement tender documents for the civil design and construction of grade separators (Road Over Bridges / Road Under Bridges) in lieu of level crossings across the Corridor-4 alignment.
OCTOBER 2025
State-Level Civic Representations
Local resident coalitions and community associations formally present detailed congestion impact data to the Hon. Deputy Chief Minister and Zonal BBMP authorities to request priority alignment for LC 136 infrastructure integration.
JANUARY 2024
Project Execution Transfer to K-RIDE
Administrative and execution responsibility for the Baiyyappanahalli-Hosur track doubling project is officially consolidated under K-RIDE to streamline regional implementation and align with the broader Bengaluru Suburban Railway Project (BSRP).
2020
Technical Review: RUB to ROB Redesign
Geotechnical engineering evaluations address severe local waterlogging and underlying lake-bed seepage risks associated with the original Road Under Bridge (RUB) proposal, necessitating a comprehensive technical redesign to a Road Over Bridge (ROB).
2018 - 2019
Track Doubling Official Sanction
The Railway Board officially sanctions the 48-km track doubling project between Baiyyappanahalli and Hosur at an initial estimated outlay of ₹498.7 crore, initiating a projected increase in regional train frequency.
2012
Initial Administrative Clearance
South Western Railway (SWR) and central authorities grant initial administrative project sanction for the Kaggadasapura LC 136 grade separation at an estimated cost of ₹31 crore under a standard municipal cost-sharing model.
Verification Sources: Right to Information (RTI) Disclosures, Public Works Documentation, Ministry of Railways Sanction Reports, and K-RIDE e-Procurement Archives.
