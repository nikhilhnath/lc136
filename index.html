LC 136 History & Official Project Timeline

A neutral retrospective chronicle of administrative milestones, technical evaluations, and infrastructure planning compiled from public records and tender disclosures.

JUNE 2026
Land Acquisition & Corridor-4 Progress
K-RIDE confirms private land acquisition for BSRP Corridor-4 is nearing final stages, supported by multi-lateral review missions from the European Investment Bank (EIB).
DECEMBER 2025
Community Representations & Escalation
As regional track doubling works intensify traffic density at LC 136, resident coalitions initiate formal petition campaigns and interface directly with the Hon. Chief Minister for priority resolution.
JANUARY 2024
Project Execution Transfer to K-RIDE
Administrative responsibility for the 48-km Baiyyappanahalli-Hosur track doubling project is officially consolidated under K-RIDE to streamline unified infrastructure integration.
2020
Technical Review: RUB to ROB Redesign
Engineering evaluations address severe local waterlogging and lake-bed seepage risks associated with the original Road Under Bridge (RUB) proposal, necessitating a comprehensive redesign to a Road Over Bridge (ROB).
2016
Initial Alignment & Property Impact Assessment
Preliminary alignment surveys identify approximately 93 property parcels required for grade separation, initiating long-term administrative feasibility reviews.
2012
Initial Administrative Project Clearance
The Central Government and Railway Ministry issue initial administrative approval for grade separation at Kaggadasapura Level Crossing LC 136.
Verification Sources: Right to Information (RTI) Disclosures, Public Works Documentation, and K-RIDE Tender Archives.
